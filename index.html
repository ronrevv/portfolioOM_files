— 02 / THE LENS BEHIND THE WORK
@OMWMOTION

LIGHT IS THE
ONLY LANGUAGE
THAT DOESN'T LIE.

OM VAISNAV is a cinematographer based in India, working at the intersection of commercial precision and documentary truth. With over five years behind the lens, his visual language is built on restraint — letting shadow speak, letting silence breathe.

From brand films for national campaigns to intimate documentary portraits, OM approaches each frame as a compositional act — every choice of light, angle, and movement in service of a deeper emotional truth. His work has screened across South Asia and earned recognition in the independent film circuit.

Trained in classical cinematography and fluent in contemporary visual grammar, OM brings both technical mastery and an artist's sensibility to every collaboration.
